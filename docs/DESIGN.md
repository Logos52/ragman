# Design: "Departures"

Ragman keeps Tarkov's layout and cell language and replaces its darkness with the visual language of an airport: departure boards, baggage tags and luggage stickers. The first real use is packing for a flight, so the chrome borrows from the trip itself. Bright white surfaces, graph-paper grids, black condensed type, one safety-orange signal color. Square corners everywhere, as in EFT. No Tarkov assets, fonts or artwork.

The three rejected directions, kept out on purpose: dark or mid-grey grounds, cream or paper textures, pastel category tints on cells. Light is the default look. Dark mode is a second token set that the owner turns on from the tab bar; see Dark.

## Composition

Desktop, 1440 px and up: tab bar 40 px across the top; then three columns, left 28%, middle 30%, right 42% (24% : 33% : 43% at 1440 px and up, where the stash never goes under its grid's width and the middle never under bag art plus a 6-cell Main grid), each a white panel with a 1 px `--line` border and 12 px gutters; the right column's header starts with the stash tabs; Quick Use row 72 px along the bottom; a 28 px status footer under it (carried weight left, save badge right).

- **Tab bar**: `--bar` strip, tabs in `--bar-ink` condensed caps 15 px, active tab in `--accent` with a 3 px `--accent` bar under it; a hovered tab stays white with a 3 px `--ink-3` bar, so only the active tab is ever accent. Right end: `PATCH NOTES`, the dark mode toggle (see Dark), then the app name "INVENTORY" in `--ink-3` (hidden under 640 px, so the four tabs and the toggle fit at 390 px).
- **Left: the person.** Loadout picker on top (segmented control, active segment `--accent`) with a `Slots` button at its right: it opens a bordered box anchored under the picker and drawn over the doll (the doll keeps its place) listing every default slot plus the loadout's custom ones as condensed caps toggles (`--accent` when in the loadout), and a `New slot` field with `Add slot`. Toggling or adding edits only the active loadout; an item in a removed slot goes home. Below it a paper doll: the figure centered, 3:6 portrait frame, slot boxes in two columns either side, Feet centered below. Left column top to bottom: Head, Eyes, Ears, Neck, Wrist. Right column: Torso Outer, Torso Mid, Torso Base, Hands, Legs, Underwear, Socks. Feet below the figure. Each slot box is 1 cell square plus a label tab (condensed caps 11 px, `--ink-2`) above its top-left edge, EFT style. Empty box: `--grid-bg` fill, a faint `--ink-3` line glyph of the slot (hat, glasses, a pair of earbuds, collar, watch, shirt, hands, trousers, briefs, sock, shoe). Equipped: the item tile fills the box. The figure is `images/avatar/base.png` with worn layers stacked in slot order when present; when there is no base image, draw the placeholder figure: a clean line silhouette in `--grid-line` with a faint white radial glow behind it. No photo, no stand-in.
- **Middle: carried.** One block per carry slot in the active loadout, in order Pockets, Backpack, Carry-on, Pouch. Pockets is a bare `pocketsGrid` panel with its label tab, and beside the tab two small `w × h` number boxes that resize it for the active loadout (pockets items that no longer fit are re-placed, else go home). A bag block: label tab with the slot name; a header row with the bag name in condensed caps and its weight readout (`1.2 kg · 3 unweighed`, plus ` · 5 est.` and a `~` before the total when any included weight is a guesstimate, plus ` / 7.0 kg` when `weightLimit_g` is set; the readout sits on the bag name's baseline; the 2 px allowance meter is a full-width line along the bottom of the whole header, taking no height of its own; the readout and the meter turn `--bad` past the limit); then the bag's own art (2 x 2 cells, left; it is the slot's drop zone) and its Main panel grid to the right. Other panels show only in the opened window. An empty carry slot is the slot box alone with the glyph.
- **Right: stash.** Header: the stash tabs, one segment per entry of `settings.locations` in order (the segmented control, active segment `--accent`; no segment is active while the Archive shows), then a 24 px `+` square that turns into an inline name field (120 px, condensed caps, 1 px `--ink` border): Enter adds the tab and opens it, Esc cancels, a blank or existing name (trimmed, any case) turns the field's border and text `--bad` and nothing is added. The tabs and the `+` sit in one row that scrolls sideways inside the header (no scrollbar drawn) when it does not fit, so the page never widens. After the row: an Archive toggle that fills `--accent` when on, and a 24 px pencil square that opens the tab editor for the open tab. The editor opens only from the pencil (a right-click on a tab does nothing), so it works the same with a mouse, a keyboard and a finger. The tab editor is the Slots editor's bordered box (1 px `--ink`, 4 px shadow), up to 360 px wide, anchored under the header over the grid, so the grid keeps its place: title `TAB · <name>` and a close `×`; a name field with `Rename` (Enter renames; the same refusals as Add, shown in `--bad` under the field); `Move left` and `Move right` (disabled at either end); `Delete` in `--bad`, disabled while any item names the tab (its location, a home in that stash, carried or archived items from there; the title says how many) and for the last tab; a `Rows` number box styled like the Pockets `w × h` boxes, with `<min> to 200 · every stash` beside it in `--ink-2`, where min is the lowest occupied row + 1 across all stashes; it applies on Enter or when it loses focus, and a value out of range turns it `--bad`. Esc closes the editor wherever the focus is, as a form; a change of screen tab closes it too. Then the right-aligned `Verify all`, `Sort` and `Add`, which drop to their own line, still right-aligned, when the tabs take the width. `Add` is `--accent` with a `+`; `Sort` auto-arranges. Second row: a search field and a sort select. While Filter by item is on, a chip sits after the search field, the search field's 24 px height (on phone it stays on the search row, the search field giving way): an `--ink` strip reading `SHOWING: <category>` (the key in `--ink-3`, the category in white condensed caps 12 px) and a close `×` that fills `--accent` on hover. The grid is `stashWidth` wide and `stashRows` tall (default 68, like EFT's fixed stash; one grid element of 3808 px at the 56 px cell) and scrolls down inside the panel; empty rows below the items are drawn to the last row. The Archive is not fixed: it draws its items plus 4 empty rows, at least 12. A vertical filter strip hugs the grid's left edge: one 32 px square per category with a line glyph, active ones in `--accent`, hover shows the name. In the Archive, every tile wears a luggage tag: a small `--ink` tag shape with a punched hole at the left and the location in white condensed caps, pinned bottom-left.
- **Quick Use**: ten boxes, each 1 cell with the digit 1 to 0 in the top-left corner in `--ink-3`, label tab "QUICK USE". Over a tile the digit sits on a small `--tile` chip in `--tile-ph-ink`, above the tile (inset 3 px, inside the selection ring, while the tile is selected); the tile fills the box exactly, so its `?` stays inside.
- **Footer**: `CARRIED 3.42 kg · 12 unweighed` (`CARRIED ~3.42 kg · 5 estimated` when any carried weight is a guesstimate) in condensed caps with a tag glyph; save badge at the right: nothing when saved, `SAVING` in `--ink-3`, `NOT SAVED · RETRY` in `--bad` on error. Right of it, 12 px apart, the sync state of the data repo: nothing when off or idle, `SYNCED 12:04` in `--ink-3` (local time of the last sync; it stays while the next sync waits for its quiet period), `PUSHING` in `--ink-3`, `PUSH FAILED · RETRY` in `--bad` (RETRY is a button that syncs now; the git message is its tooltip), `CONFLICT · PULL BY HAND · RETRY` in `--bad` (pull the data repo by hand, then RETRY syncs now; the server's conflict flag picks this badge, not the message text). When the weight and both badges do not fit in one row (phone, both in error), the badges wrap onto a second row, right-aligned, and the footer grows; the page never scrolls sideways. The public build shows nothing.

## Cells and tiles

- Grid: `--grid-bg` fill with 1 px `--grid-line` lines, cells `--cell` square. Rendered as one element with a repeating-linear-gradient background, items absolutely positioned on top. Crisp lines, no sub-pixel blur.
- Item tile: `--tile` fill, 1 px `--tile-line` border, the art letterboxed inside with 2 px padding, `object-fit: contain`. Name label in the top-right, condensed 10 px `--strip-ink` on the `--strip` (white 85%), truncated with an ellipsis (dropped on small tiles, below). A rotated item rotates its art 90° (`transform: rotate(90deg)`), never re-drawn.
- Container tile: double rule, 1 px `--strip-ink` border plus a 1 px `--strip-ink` outline inset 2 px, so a bag reads as a bag at any size. Its label strip is `--bar` with `--bar-ink` text.
- Placeholder tile (no art): `--tile-ph` fill, the item name centered in condensed caps `--tile-ph-ink`, wrapped, 11 px. Never an image of a similar item.
- Reserved region: 1.5 px dashed `--region` outline on the grid, under the items, with a caps label in `--region` (9 px; 8 px at the 34 px phone cell) on a `--region-chip` (white 85%), centred on the region's bottom edge 2 px inside it, drawn above the items (`pointer-events: none`). Bottom-centre is the one spot a tile draws nothing (name top-right, marks top-left, `?` and tag bottom-left, quantity and care bottom-right), so the label, the name strip and the `?` never overlap at any cell size. The label keeps 14 px clear at each side and truncates with an ellipsis rather than reach a corner mark.
- Special care: a tiny `--accent` corner triangle bottom-right of the tile.
- Top-left mark row, EFT style (the name strip shortens to make room): pin glyph (solid) when `pinned` (Sort leaves it in place), lock glyph (outline, keyhole showing) when `locked`, each in `--strip-ink` on its own `--tile` chip with a faint hairline so it reads over any art; then the consumable's `--strip-ink` dot with a `--tile` ring, on its own chip like the pin and lock. The name strip shortens by the whole mark row plus 4 px. Glyph size follows the cell: 14 px at 56, 12 px at 48, 10 px at 34 (`--rg-mk = clamp(10px, --cell / 4, 14px)`). A locked tile shows the default cursor and never starts a drag: a drag attempt flashes it `--bad` once (80 ms) and nothing moves.
- Unverified (EFT's unexamined mark): a 12 px `--accent` square with a white condensed bold `?`, bottom-left, whenever `verified` is false or absent. It sits above the luggage tag in the Archive (top-right on a small tile). Ticking Verified on the card removes it. The stash header's `VERIFY ALL` button (check glyph, before Sort; its title says how many are unverified) ticks every item in the inventory, all locations, the Archive and everything carried, as one undo step; once none is left it reads `UNVERIFY ALL` and clears them all.
- Stack quantity: when `quantity` > 1, the count in condensed bold 11 px `--strip-ink` on the `--strip`, bottom-right (left of the special-care triangle when both are drawn). A quantity of 1 or absent draws nothing.
- Small tiles (rendered width under 40 px, a container query on the tile: a 1-cell-wide tile at the 34 px phone cell; desktop 1x1 tiles at 56 and 48 px keep their name strip, shortened past the mark row; a 1-cell-wide desktop tile with two or more marks drops it instead, since only a letter or two would be left). Priority, highest first: the art, the marks, the luggage tag, the name. The name strip is dropped (the name is in the tooltip and on the Inspect card; a placeholder still centres its name), the marks sit flush in the top-left corner at their cell size, the `?` and the quantity shrink to 10 px, and the luggage tag goes compact (flush bottom-left, 11 px). When the tag holds the bottom-left, the `?` moves to the top-right and the quantity rises above the tag, so no two of them overlap.
- Container nickname: when set, it replaces the name on the `--ink` label strip. A placeholder container (no art) still centers its real name and draws the nickname on the strip.
- Hover: tile border `--ink`. Selected (after click): 2 px `--accent` ring; inside a slot or Quick Use box the ring is drawn inset over the tile's edge, so all four sides show, and the `?` and the top-left marks step 3 px in (the `?` with a 1 px white edge) so they never run into the ring.
- Drag (the EFT way): the ghost is the tile at 0.6 opacity, `scale(1.03)`, no shadow, following the pointer via `transform`. The target cells are lit on the grid layer, under the ghost, so they read through it: each cell fills `--ok` at 35% with a 2 px `--ok` border when the drop fits, `--bad` at 35% with a 2 px `--bad` border when it does not (a slot box lights the same way). A drop that would merge onto an identical stack lights that stack's own cells `--ok`. Drop snaps in 80 ms ease-out; a failed drop snaps back in 80 ms with a `--bad` flash on the source tile. Nothing else animates.
- Drag modifiers, as in EFT. Ctrl held over a container's TILE (not its grid) quick-moves into it (first free cells of its first panel that accepts the item); the container tile fills `--ok` at 35% with a 2 px `--ok` outline inset when it has room, `--bad` when it does not, and a `--bad` drop snaps back. Shift at the start of a drag of a stack (quantity > 1) takes exactly one unit: the ghost draws no count, the source keeps the rest, and dropping it back on its own stack is refused. Dropping a stack onto an identical stack (same product, both consumable) merges them into the target.
- Keys and clicks: Delete (or Backspace) on the selected item opens its menu at the tile on the delete confirm. Double-click opens a container's window and a plain item's Inspect window.

## Overlays

- **Tooltip**: `--bar` box, `--bar-ink` text, 12 px body, 160 ms delay, placed below-right of the pointer (left of it near the right edge), following it; it never covers a reserved-region label or the hovered tile's own name strip: when below would, it flips above the pointer, and when above would too, it sits beside the pointer, vertically centred on it (right, else left). Lines: name (condensed caps 13 px), status chip, `2 × 2`, note.
- **Context menu**: `--panel`, 1 px `--ink` border, 4 px `--shadow`, condensed caps 13 px rows, hover `--accent` with `--accent-ink` text. It keeps the windows' 16 px gutter from every viewport edge. A submenu opens to the right of its row, or to the left when only that side fits; when neither side has room (a phone) it stacks in the menu's own flow under its row, exactly the menu's width, pushing the rows below down (never covering them): a 3 px `--accent` top rule, a 1 px `--line` rule under it, rows indented 20 px, long rows truncated with an ellipsis. Order: Open (containers only), Inspect, Filter by item, Equip to slot (submenu of fitting slots), Attach to (submenu of `<parent> · <slot>`, `(swap)` when taken; only when some attachment slot takes the item) or Detach (attached items), Move to Stash, Archive or Unarchive, Add to Quick Use, Pin or Unpin, Lock or Unlock, Mark verified or Mark unverified, Rename (containers only), Split stack (stacks of 2 or more only), Edit, Delete (in `--bad`, always last). On a locked item the rows that would move it (Equip, Attach, Detach, Move to Stash, Archive, Split, Delete) are disabled in `--ink-3`.
  - Filter by item: every item that does the same job (`sameJob`: same category, and the same tag set when the item has tags) stays lit and everything else on every grid sits under a `--dim` veil (in light it matches 25% opacity), until Esc, the chip's `×`, or a click on empty space.
  - Rename and Split stack turn their row into an inline field: the row label, a 22 px input with a 1 px `--ink` border (`--accent` ring when focused), and an `--accent` `OK`. Rename edits the nickname (blank removes it); Split takes a count from 1 to quantity − 1, prefilled with 1 and selected so Enter works at once (`of N` beside it in `--ink-2`); the row keeps the menu's 12 px right padding and the new stack lands in the first free cells of the same grid. Enter applies, Esc returns to the menu.
  - Delete asks inline: `Delete <name>?` in `--ink` (a prompt, not a disabled row; `--ink-3` is for disabled rows only), `Yes, delete` in `--bad`, `No`. A locked item answers `<name> is locked · Unlock to delete` instead.
- **Inspect window**: a movable window (not a modal), the container window's frame with a 6 px `--accent` bar under the title bar (`INSPECT · <name>`), the card 720 px wide inside. Several can be open at once; each new one cascades 28 px from the last and is 28 px shorter per step (its cap is the room under the tab bar less its cascade offset), so a tall card is never clamped back over the one beneath it and the cascade shows on a phone too, and they share one stacking order with the container windows (click raises). Esc closes the top one once no menu, tooltip or form is open. Left: the photo on `--grid-bg` (or the placeholder); on phone the card stacks and the photo (and the Edit form's paste zone) spans the card's width, square. Right: name (condensed 24 px), nameEn under it when set, status chip, then a two-column field list in body 13 px: maker (linked), category, location, size, color, footprint, weight, tags, special-care badge, consumable flag, note, lesson, attachments (slot: item), panels (containers only). Blank fields render as blank value cells, never hidden and never filled with placeholder words. `hidden.*` is never on the card. Under the chips: a `Verified` checkbox (`--accent` tick) with the `?` mark beside it while unticked; chips add `Pinned` (outline) and `Locked` (`--ink`). A container's card adds a Nickname row, a consumable's a Quantity row (absent counts as 1). A container's card also shows its panels as live grids under the fields (label `CONTENTS`), the same rendering as its container window, so items drag in and out from there.
- **Edit form**: stays a modal over everything; the same card with inputs; on desktop the photo column (paste zone) is sticky at the top of the card's scroller, so the photo stays in view while the fields and Panels scroll; its close `×` sits in the card's top-right corner above the photo (on phone the card keeps 40 px of top padding so the stacked paste zone starts under the `×`, never over it); a paste zone that accepts a pasted image (saved to `images/photos/<slug>.png` through the store) and sets `images.photo`. A container adds `Weight limit (g)` (the airline allowance the header measures against); a consumable adds `Quantity` (1 or blank = absent). Under the fields, `Panels`: one bordered box per panel with its name, `w × h`, accepts (blank = anything) and a remove `×`, its reserved regions indented behind a dashed `--region` rule (name, `at x y`, `w × h`, accepts, `×`); the fields wrap among themselves and the `×` keeps its own column on the row's first line, `+ Region` and `+ Panel` buttons, and a `Layout` select (Row or Column). An item with no panels becomes a container by adding one. A bad panel list (blank or duplicate name, a region outside its grid) shows a `--bad` line and disables Save. Add item uses the same form; only the name is required (paste, name, save; a blank category stays blank); it saves the item as 1x1 in the current stash.
- **Attachments on the card**: each `slot: item` row has a small `Detach` button (disabled on a locked item). Dropping an item on the tile of an item whose attachment slot takes it (its tags or category include the slot name: a band on the watch) attaches it; the parent tile lights `--ok` like a Ctrl-drag target.
- **Container window**: 1 px `--ink` border, a 28 px `--bar` title bar with the bag name in `--bar-ink` condensed caps, its weight readout and a close `×`; draggable by the title bar; windows stack and raise on click; the panels inside sit in a row (bags) or a column (`panelLayout: column`, drawer units), each with its label tab and grid; panels that are empty and not the main one collapse behind a `+` chip that expands them; the chips sit together in one wrapping group after the shown panels, at most 6 cells wide (full width on phone), so a bag with many pockets stays compact. The title leads with the nickname when one is set (`<nickname> · <name>`).
- **Windows and the tab bar**: no window goes above the 40 px tab bar, and a window is capped at the viewport height below it (its body scrolls), so a tall container Inspect window never covers the tabs.
- **Window gutter**: every window keeps a 16 px gutter from the left, right and bottom viewport edges and from the tab bar, on open, while dragging, on resize and whenever its own size changes; it is never wider than the viewport less 32 px (its panels wrap) nor taller than the room under the tab bar less 32 px.
- **Window memory**: container and Inspect windows reopen where this viewer last dropped them, remembered per item id in the browser's local storage (a per-viewer convenience; when storage is blocked they open at the cascade). A remembered spot is clamped so the window stays inside the viewport, and windows are re-clamped on resize.

## States

Every view has four. Loading: the grid skeleton (empty grids, no items) for at most one frame, no spinner; it carries the loadout picker row (two blank segments and a disabled `Slots` button), the stash header's tabs as two blank disabled segments of their width with a disabled `+`, and a disabled pencil after Archive, and the filter strip's column as blank disabled squares (ten, the default category count: before the file loads the categories are unknown and nothing is guessed, so an inventory with another count shows more or fewer squares when it arrives; the strip's column and row keep their size, so nothing else moves), so the doll, slots, header and grid do not move when the data arrives. ## Dark

A second token set for the same layout. It keeps the cool blue-grey Departures greys; the grid cells are lighter than the panel, as EFT's stash cells are.

- **Toggle**: an icon button in the tab bar's right end, before `INVENTORY`: a moon in light mode, a sun in dark mode (both line glyphs in `glyphs.tsx`), in `--ink-3`, `--bar-ink` on hover, `--accent` while dark mode is on. A fixed 24 px box, so it never moves the tabs. `aria-pressed` is true in dark mode; the title reads `Dark mode` or `Light mode`. It is a normal button, reached with Tab.
- **State**: `data-theme="dark"` on `<html>`; absent means light, the default. Kept per browser in `localStorage` under `ragman:theme` (`dark`, or no key for light), every read and write in try/catch: blocked storage means light. An inline script at the top of `index.html` applies the stored value before first paint, and a small inline style gives the page the dark `--page` fill before the stylesheet loads, so there is no light flash. `src/ui/theme.ts` reads, applies, stores and toggles it; `useTheme()` gives React the current theme. The dark block also sets `color-scheme: dark`, so native selects, checkboxes and scrollbars match.
- **Tiles stay light.** Product photos have white backgrounds, so `--tile` stays a light grey and everything drawn on a tile uses tile-side tokens: the name and quantity strips (`--strip`, `--strip-ink`), the hover border and container double rule (`--strip-ink`), the pin, lock and dot chips (`--tile` fill, `--strip-ink` glyph), and the Quick Use digit chip. Placeholder tiles (no art) are light too (`--tile-ph`), so every tile reads as the same kind of object. Text never uses `--ink` on a tile in dark mode (1.03:1).
- **Ink strips stay dark.** The tab bar, tooltip, window title bars, luggage tags, the container label strip, the strong label tab and the `SHOWING` chip use `--bar` and `--bar-ink` in both themes. The ink button and the `Locked` chip keep `--ink` with `--accent-ink`, which in dark mode is a light fill with dark text.
- **Status chips**: text `#14171C` on Daily, Rotation, Seasonal and Not Worn, white on Travel Only, `--ink` on Retired. The reserved-region label sits on a `--panel` chip (`--region-chip`), because on the light strip `--region` measures 2.93:1.
